### **How Does Creatine Work?**

Creatine Monohydrate is a naturally occurring compound that helps to replenish your ATP levels, the energy currency for your muscles. When you’re lifting heavy weights, sprinting, or performing other high-intensity exercises, your muscles quickly deplete their ATP stores. Supplementing with creatine helps to keep your muscles charged with ATP, allowing you to push harder, recover faster, and build more muscle over time.
